About PakRozgar

PakRozgar is Pakistan's daily job update website. Our mission is simple: bring every important job advertisement - from government departments, banks, armed forces, and private companies - to one place, in easy language, updated every single day.

We collect job ads from Pakistan's major newspapers (Jang, Express, Dawn, Nawa-i-Waqt) and official department websites, then present them with clear details: eligibility criteria, vacancies, salary, last date, and step-by-step application guidance.

What we publish daily:

  • Government jobs (federal & provincial)
  • Bank jobs
  • Army, Police and forces recruitment
  • Teaching and health department jobs
  • Private sector and IT jobs

New job posts are added 1-3 times daily. Bookmark PakRozgar and never miss an opportunity.
